### Abstract

Let a compact connected Lie group $G$ act on a compact symplectic manifold $M$ with moment map $\mu$, and let $M/\!/G=\mu^{-1}(0)/G$ be the symplectic reduction. Kirwan proved that, when $0$ is a regular value of $\mu$, the restriction map $H^*_G(M;\mathbb{Q})\to H^*_G(\mu^{-1}(0);\mathbb{Q})\cong H^*(M/\!/G;\mathbb{Q})$ is onto, and for torus actions Tolman and Weitsman described its kernel. Both results are over $\mathbb{Q}$. Their proofs use the Atiyah–Bott lemma, which fails over $\mathbb{Z}$, and over $\mathbb{Z}$ the last isomorphism needs a free action.

In this thesis we propose that there should be a chromatic Kirwan theory, that is, a Kirwan theory in Morava $K$-theory $K(n)$ for each prime $p$ and each height $n\geq 1$, whose answers may depend on $n$.

Most of the thesis is a detailed account of Kirwan theory over $\mathbb{Q}$. It shows that almost all of the classical argument works in any complex-oriented theory, and that the one exception is the Atiyah–Bott lemma. Pomerleano and Teleman proved this lemma in Morava $K$-theory and deduced Kirwan surjectivity over $\mathbb{Z}$ for free actions. Using their work and that of Bai and Pomerleano, we prove the Tolman–Weitsman description of the kernel in $K(n)$ and $K_{p^r}(n)$ for torus actions. Going back to $\mathbb{Z}$ likewise, we show that for a torus acting freely on $\mu^{-1}(0)$ the integral kernel is generated by classes vanishing on half spaces, which Tolman and Weitsman proved in special cases, and Harada and Landweber in $K$-theory. This also recovers the integral cohomology of polygon spaces. We end with a discussion of what a chromatic Kirwan theory could be.

### Contents

0. Introduction
1. **[Equivariant Cohomology](/posts/equivariant-cohomology/).** The Borel construction; genuine and Borel equivariance; Borel–Moore homology and Euler classes; complex projective space; the Cartan model and localisation; fixed points and GKM theory.
